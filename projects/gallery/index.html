Approach

The gallery serves as a rapid experimentation ground, enabling stakeholders to experience applied behavioral science ideas before full implementation. By iterating in the open, we collect feedback on usability, investigative value, and integration needs.

Each prototype is scaffolded with brief guides and cognitive walkthroughs to help teams envision deployment within investigative, intelligence, or training pipelines.
