Core Competencies

My professional qualifications demonstrate an interdisciplinary foundation in clinical psychology, quantitative research, and mission-driven service. These capabilities, honed through direct field experience and academic training, enable me to support evidence-informed initiatives in various investigative, institutional, and analytical settings.

Behavioral Analysis & Investigative Support

Rooted in formal psychological training and clinical work, I bring behavioral insight that informs risk evaluation, threat assessment, and mission-relevant decision-making.

  • Clinical Assessment: Participated in multidisciplinary evaluations of individuals in psychiatric crisis—documenting clinical observations and mental status findings.
  • Crisis Response & De-escalation: Trained in verbal de-escalation, suicide prevention, and stabilization techniques within high-acuity psychiatric settings.
  • Psychological Models: Academic background in cognitive-behavioral models with practical understanding of their relevance to structured interviews and mental health.

Quantitative Analysis & Strategic Insight

Analytical competencies were developed through undergraduate and graduate-level research, emphasizing methodological rigor and policy-relevant interpretation.

  • Statistical Programming: Proficient in statistical software for modeling, hypothesis testing, and data management—applied to psychological, health, and public-sector data.
  • Data Interpretation & Communication: Skilled in deriving evidence-based conclusions from complex datasets and communicating findings through written reports.
  • Methodological Integrity: Trained in research design, measurement, and ethics, ensuring all analytical work is scientifically valid and reproducible.

Operational Readiness & Professional Integrity

Experienced in environments requiring discretion, composure, and collaborative execution in support of high-stakes institutional objectives.

  • Confidentiality & Compliance: Regularly handled protected health information under HIPAA and institutional privacy protocols, maintaining strict adherence to legal and ethical standards.
  • Interdisciplinary Collaboration: Worked closely with psychiatrists, clinical psychologists, nursing staff, and administrative personnel to implement coordinated plans.
  • Mission-Driven Service: Committed to public-sector impact through principled, data-informed work supporting accountability and institutional trust.
