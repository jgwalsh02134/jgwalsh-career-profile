About Me

My mission is to apply behavioral science and data-driven insights to enhance public safety and organizational effectiveness.

I am a psychology graduate with a deep-seated interest in the intersection of human behavior, data, and public service. My academic and professional journey has been driven by a desire to understand the "why" behind human actions and to use that understanding to contribute to safer, more effective organizations and communities.

My background includes clinical experience in high-acuity psychiatric settings, where I honed skills in crisis intervention, de-escalation, and clinical assessment. This hands-on work provided me with invaluable insights into complex human behaviors and the critical importance of empathy, precision, and composure under pressure.

Complementing my clinical background is a strong foundation in quantitative research and statistical analysis. I am proficient in using tools like Python and SPSS to analyze data, identify patterns, and generate actionable insights. I believe that rigorous, evidence-based analysis is essential for addressing systemic challenges and informing sound policy and strategy.

I am committed to a career in the public sector, where I can apply my unique blend of skills to support investigative work, enhance operational integrity, and contribute to data-informed decision-making.

Areas of Focus

My work is centered on three interconnected domains where psychology, data, and direct action converge.

Behavioral Analysis & Assessment

Applying psychological principles to understand behavior, assess risk, and support investigative functions. My approach is grounded in clinical observation and evidence-based models.

Data Analysis & Research

Using quantitative tools like SPSS, R, and Python to analyze data, test hypotheses, and translate complex findings into clear, actionable intelligence for strategic decision-making.

Crisis Intervention & Response

Trained and experienced in crisis prevention, de-escalation, and suicide prevention within high-acuity settings. Focused on maintaining safety, stability, and composure under pressure.
