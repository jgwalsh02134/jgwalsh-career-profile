Albany Crime Dashboard

2024 Live #civic-data #dashboards #analysis

The Albany Crime Dashboard centralizes incident-level data, translating CSV exports into interactive charts, heatmaps, and trend comparisons. It gives city analysts, journalists, and neighborhood advocates a clear view of how violent and property crime changes across wards, districts, and time horizons.

Approach

The dashboard pipelines open data from Albany PD, normalizes historical boundary changes, and stitches incident-level records with contextual demographic indicators. A lightweight governance kit ensures analysts can refresh and publish quarterly updates in under an hour.

Upcoming work focuses on predictive risk layering, integration with 311 calls, and adding storytelling modules that guide residents through key findings.
