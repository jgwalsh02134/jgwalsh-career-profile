Behavioral Threat Triage

This project simulates the first step a behavioral threat-assessment team would take when a threatening message is received. It scans the text for validated risk factors (BTAM, TRAP-18, HCR-20, lexical aggression, protective cues), assigns a banded risk level, and produces a clear action plan: immediate, next 24–72h, and follow-up steps. The goal is to show how psychology and structured professional judgment can be translated into transparent, auditable decision-support tools for law enforcement and security contexts.
