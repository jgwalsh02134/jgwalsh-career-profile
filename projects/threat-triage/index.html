Behavioral Threat Triage

2025 Live #investigative #risk #psychology

Behavioral Threat Triage codifies structured professional judgment for intake teams. The prototype ingests a narrative, highlights BTAM, TRAP-18, and HCR-20 indicators, and surfaces an auditable action plan that separates immediate tasks from 24–72 hour follow-ups. The objective is to show how applied psychology can power transparent decision-support tools for law enforcement and workplace safety units.

Approach

The intake engine draws from case law, Secret Service behavioral threat research, and structured professional judgment models. Deterministic rules ensure repeatability, while diagnostics capture when generative AI assists are invoked, allowing supervisors to audit outputs.

The roadmap introduces joint analyst review, cross-case comparisons, and integrations with guardian follow-up teams—extending deterministic triage into a full-spectrum threat management workflow.
