        Case Study (STAR)

        Situation

        Agencies receive threatening narratives that require rapid, transparent triage. This prototype structures observable indicators into a defensible first-line screen.

        Task

        • Highlight behavioral indicators
        • Produce a banded score with suggested actions
        • Keep the process auditable and ethically framed

        Action

        • Implemented deterministic rules (BTAM, TRAP-18 subset, HCR-20 context)
        • Explainability toggle to reveal rubric JSON
        • Validation plan: inter-rater reliability; small labeled set; perturbation tests

        Result

        • Structured, auditable intake prototype aligned to federal threat-assessment practice
        • Clear limits & escalation guidance

        Limits & Ethics

        • Signals are indicators—not proof, diagnosis, or profiling.
        • Human review mandatory. Avoid PII in demos.
        • Use snapshots & versioned rubrics for transparency.

        References (for recruiters)

        • Behavioral Threat Assessment & Management (BTAM) cross-sector guides and DHS/USSS practice documents.
        • TRAP-18 warning behaviors (fixation, leakage, pathway, identification, novel aggression, energy burst, last resort).
        • HCR-20 (historical/clinical/risk) family of contextual factors used in forensic risk assessment.
        • MOAS and Buss–Perry aggression measures (for illustration only; not diagnostic).
